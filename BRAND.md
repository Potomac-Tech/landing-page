# Potomac website brand identity

## Brand idea

**The Lunar Field Brief** — mission operations crossed with a premium scientific atlas.

Potomac should feel like the intelligence layer is already operating: precise, calm, evidence-led, and commercially literate. The brand is not generic space futurism. It is the moment uncertainty becomes ground truth.

## Positioning

- Brand promise: **Building the market that builds the Moon.**
- Category: **Leaders of the Lunar Data Market.**
- Hero idea: **Before you build the Moon, scout the ground.**
- Product idea: **Buy the answer. Not a mission.**
- Pathfinder platform: **The CubeSat for the Moon.** Lower-cost hardware access opens the lunar market to universities, startups, and established operators.
- Brand behavior: **Send the scout first.**

## Visual system

| Token          | Value     | Role                                   |
| -------------- | --------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Obsidian       | `#000000` | Primary field, authority, depth        |
| Carbon         | `#11151A` | Elevated dark surfaces                 |
| Graphite       | `#34383D` | Secondary structure                    |
| Moonstone      | `#ECEBE5` | Reading surfaces and primary type      |
| Fog            | `#A8AAA7` | Secondary copy and metadata            |
| Signal brass   | `#C5A24B` | Primary emphasis and wayfinding        |
| Dark brass     | `#806E3C` | Emphasis on light surfaces             |
| Telemetry cyan | `#2FCDFF` | Live observations and active data only |

Use a true-black field throughout the site. Brass should behave like a signal—not decoration. Cyan is reserved for data becoming active.

## Typography

- **Geist Sans:** headlines, body copy, navigation, and utility text.
- **Cormorant Garamond:** human emphasis, key conclusions, and the Potomac heritage voice.
- **Geist Mono:** coordinates, indices, metrics, and telemetry.

Headlines are declarative and tightly set. Metadata is small, uppercase, and widely tracked. Body copy remains sentence case and readable.

## Logo

Retain the swan, crescent, star, and river emblem. It connects the name Potomac to heritage and place while avoiding generic space-company symbolism.

- Use a one-color moonstone mark on dark surfaces.
- Keep the emblem and wordmark optically balanced; do not compress the crest.
- Use the authentic full Potomac lockup in navigation and brand moments.
- Avoid gray tiles, bevels, drop shadows, and metallic gradients in final production lockups.

## Image direction

Choose tactile lunar terrain, field hardware, true-scale mapping, and authentic operator photography. Favor low-key exposure, sharp relief, mineral texture, and restrained warm rim light. Label concept imagery clearly.

Avoid generic astronauts, glossy sci-fi control rooms, excessive gold, and decorative space imagery that does not explain the product.

## Layout and motion

- Use severe asymmetric splits and confident negative space.
- Structure pages with fine rules, concise labels, coordinates, and restrained reticles.
- Keep the page grounded on uninterrupted black surfaces; never add a decorative grid pattern.
- Animation should reveal evidence or orientation. Avoid ambient spectacle and perpetual motion.

## Voice

Potomac is assured, specific, and concise. It names the decision, the missing evidence, and the next action. It does not overclaim.

Prefer: “Local observations from the sites where missions will operate.”

Avoid: “Revolutionary next-generation solutions transforming the future of space.”

Any date-sensitive traction, proposal value, launch timing, or future network claim should be labeled and refreshed before external publication.
